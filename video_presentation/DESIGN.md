# Orderfox Video Design Identity

## Style Prompt
A premium, dark-first cinematic presentation for Orderfox. The aesthetic is "Obsidian Tech" — deep blacks, sharp glassmorphism cards, and high-energy orange accents. Motion is fast, responsive, and fluid, reflecting the efficiency of the ordering system.

## Colors
- **Obsidian Background**: `#050505`
- **Orderfox Orange**: `#F2460D` (Primary Accent)
- **Glass Surface**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`
- **Border Light**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`
- **Muted Text**: `#A1A1AA`

## Typography
- **Headlines**: "Inter", sans-serif (Extra Bold, 800)
- **Body**: "Inter", sans-serif (Medium, 500)

## Motion Signature
- **Easing**: `expo.out` for entrances, `power4.inOut` for transitions.
- **Vibe**: High-end SaaS, sleek, professional.

## What NOT to Do
- No generic blues or greens.
- No rounded corners below 12px (keep it soft but modern).
- No jump cuts (use shader or wipe transitions).
- No cluttered frames.
